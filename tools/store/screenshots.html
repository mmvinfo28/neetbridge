How neetbridge works

Detect

Your own Submit button

The extension watches the site's own submission call and picks up the code, the language and the verdict. Nothing to copy, nothing to configure.

Translate

973 problems mapped

NeetCode renames problems and methods (two-integer-sum → two-sum, hasDuplicate → containsDuplicate). The mapping and signature fixes are built in and verified against both sites.

Deliver

Verdict where you are

Submitted with your own session, spaced out to respect rate limits. The result shows up as a toast on the page and as a desktop notification.
